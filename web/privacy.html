Rocky Run – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Developer: Jovita Sutanto

Rocky Run is a free endless-runner game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on Android, the web or Windows.

Information we collect

None. Rocky Run does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few settings only on your own device so they are remembered next time: your best score, music and sound volume, and your language choice. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app (or clear the site data in your browser).

Permissions

The Android app does not request any special permissions.

Children

Rocky Run does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
